Why this exists

Workplace language is often polite, compressed, and ambiguous. This tiny tool turns common phrases into clearer intent and a practical next step. It is deliberately lightweight: no login, no tracking, and no meeting recording. Known phrases stay in your browser; unknown phrases can use the optional AI decoder.

How it works

1.Enter a phrase from a meeting, email, or chat.
2.Known phrases use the local dictionary; new phrases can fall back to AI.
3.You get plain English + a useful next action.
